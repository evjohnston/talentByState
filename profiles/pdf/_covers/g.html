Cover G: postal code in caps

The postal code at poster scale, placed like option C and cut by the page edge, drawn in small mortarboards. The count is the international share of the state's STEM master's degrees (48 per 100 in California, 62 in Texas, 33 in DC), shown either as bright caps among dim ones or as gaps where caps are missing, scattered or grouped to one side.
